What We Deliver

Network Services

Every component below is deployed with documented change control, tested against a defined acceptance checklist, and handed off with full configuration backups stored in Ninja Docs.

🌐

Hardware Refresh & Core Infrastructure

Full replacement of all end-of-life switching and routing hardware with Cisco Catalyst 9200 series switches and ISR/8300 routers at every site. Meraki access points replace aging wireless infrastructure, providing centralized cloud management and consistent RF policies across all locations. Every device is racked, cabled, and configured to a documented baseline before cutover.

🔒

Redundancy & Failover

Active/standby firewall HA pairs at the primary site eliminate single points of failure at the perimeter. Secondary ISP links provide automatic failover so an outage at your primary carrier is transparent to end users. Site-to-site VPN tunnels encrypt all inter-site traffic and reroute automatically on link failure, keeping remote offices connected even when the primary path is down.

📊

Monitoring & Alerting

PRTG Network Monitor provides real-time visibility into all seven sites — device health, bandwidth utilization, latency, and uptime. Threshold-based alerts route to the NOC and automatically generate a Freshdesk ticket so no alert sits unacknowledged. NinjaOne generates weekly patch compliance reports every Monday, giving your team a clear view of endpoint health without manual effort.

📝

Reporting & Documentation

Monthly executive reports cover SLA performance, CSAT scores, ticket trends, patch compliance, and any upcoming planned work. Network topology diagrams, device configuration backups, and change logs are maintained in Ninja Docs and updated after every change window. Quarterly technology reviews include a forward-looking roadmap with budget implications for the next 12 months.
