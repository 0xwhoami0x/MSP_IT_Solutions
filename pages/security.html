Our Process

Security Assessment

Every engagement begins with a comprehensive security assessment of your current environment. Our methodology covers discovery, gap analysis, and risk-ranked reporting — the result is a clear picture of your exposure and a prioritized remediation roadmap mapped to business impact.

  • Review network perimeter and internal segmentation for exposure risks
  • Assess server and service patch levels against current CVE database
  • Audit identity and access management for privilege creep and orphaned accounts
  • Evaluate email security posture — SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and anti-phishing controls
  • Review backup systems for coverage gaps, retention policy, and tested restore capability
  • Analyze LAN/WAN traffic for lateral movement indicators and unencrypted data flows
