IT Security Assessment

Every engagement begins with a comprehensive security assessment of your current environment. Our methodology covers discovery, gap analysis, and risk-ranked reporting — the result is a clear picture of your exposure and a prioritized remediation roadmap mapped to business impact.

  • Review network perimeter and internal segmentation for exposure risks
  • Assess server and service patch levels against current CVE database
  • Audit identity and access management for privilege creep and orphaned accounts
  • Evaluate email security posture — SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and anti-phishing controls
  • Review backup systems for coverage gaps, retention policy, and tested restore capability
  • Analyze LAN/WAN traffic for lateral movement indicators and unencrypted data flows

What We Deliver

Security Services

Every security control is documented with a defined owner, a tested runbook, and a monthly review cycle. Nothing goes unmonitored and nothing goes unpatched without a tracked exception.

🛡️

SIEM & Threat Detection

Wazuh SIEM is deployed as the central log aggregation and correlation engine for all seven sites. Every firewall, server, endpoint, and network device ships logs to Wazuh in real time. Correlation rules fire automated alerts on known attack patterns — brute force attempts, lateral movement, privilege escalation, and data exfiltration indicators. Every alert generates a Freshdesk ticket within 60 seconds and is triaged by the on-call security specialist. Monthly threat summaries are included in your executive report.

💾

Backup & Disaster Recovery

Veeam Backup & Replication protects every virtual machine with nightly full or incremental backups. Local retention runs 30 days on-site at the co-location facility for fast recovery. Offsite retention extends to 90 days at a geographically separate facility for ransomware and disaster recovery scenarios. Recovery procedures are documented per workload and tested quarterly — we verify the backup, not just the backup job. RTO and RPO targets are defined per application tier and reflected in the SLA.

📧

Email Security & Phishing Defense

Microsoft 365 Business Premium includes Defender for Office 365, providing advanced threat protection for email with Safe Links, Safe Attachments, and anti-phishing policies tuned to your domain. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records are configured and monitored for all outbound domains. Simulated phishing campaigns are run quarterly to measure user susceptibility and target training to the highest-risk individuals. Results are reported in the monthly executive briefing alongside click rates and training completion.

👤

Identity & Access Protection

Azure AD Identity Protection monitors all user accounts for risky sign-in behavior — impossible travel, leaked credential alerts, and anomalous access patterns. Conditional access policies enforce MFA on every external authentication and block non-compliant devices from accessing corporate resources. Privileged Identity Management limits standing admin access — elevated permissions require just-in-time activation with approval and full audit logging. Quarterly access reviews identify and remove orphaned accounts and excess permissions.

📋

Patch Management & Compliance

NinjaOne enforces a defined patch policy across all 592 Windows endpoints — critical OS and application patches are deployed within 72 hours of release, standard patches roll out on a defined monthly schedule. JAMF Pro manages patch compliance for 57 MacBooks and 89 iPads on the same schedule. Patch compliance reports are generated every Monday and reviewed in the weekly operations call. Any exception requires a documented risk acceptance signed by your IT Manager. CIS benchmark compliance is assessed quarterly across all managed systems.

Security Reporting

Monthly executive reports include a threat summary, vulnerability status, patch compliance rate, phishing simulation results, and any security incidents from the prior month. Every incident report includes a documented timeline, root cause analysis, and remediation steps taken. Quarterly security reviews assess your program against the current threat landscape and produce an updated risk register with prioritized remediation work for the next quarter.
