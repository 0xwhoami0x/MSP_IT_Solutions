What We Deliver

Help Desk Services

Every service component below is staffed by a named specialist, governed by a defined SLA tier, and reported monthly in your executive briefing. Nothing falls through the cracks.

🖥️

Ticketing & SLA Management

Freshdesk is the single system of record for every request, incident, change, and problem across all seven sites. SLA rules auto-escalate any ticket approaching its response deadline and notify the shift lead before a breach occurs. Every ticket closes with a CSAT survey sent to the end user. Monthly reports surface ticket volume, response time performance, resolution rate, and top recurring issue categories so we can proactively eliminate repeat problems.

💻

Windows Endpoint Management

NinjaOne RMM manages all 592 Windows endpoints across your seven sites. Remote access allows technicians to connect to any device for troubleshooting without dispatching onsite. Automated patch deployment handles OS and application updates on a defined schedule, with compliance reports generated every Monday. Scripted remediations automatically resolve common issues — disk cleanup, service restarts, profile fixes — without requiring a ticket to be manually worked.

🍎

Apple Device Management

JAMF Pro manages 57 MacBooks and 89 iPads with zero-touch enrollment through Apple Business Manager. New devices ship directly to employees and enroll automatically when powered on — no IT staff required on-site for setup. Configuration profiles enforce FileVault encryption, screen lock policies, and approved application lists. Software updates are pushed on schedule with deferral controls. Lost or stolen devices are remotely locked and wiped within minutes.

📷

Thin Client & VDI Management

Citrix manages desktop delivery for all 138 thin clients across your sites. Virtual desktops stream from the co-located environment, keeping processing centralized and thin client hardware requirements minimal. vDisk updates apply to every thin client on the next reboot — there's no per-device patching cycle. The thin client fleet has a significantly longer replacement lifecycle than full PCs, reducing capital expenditure and on-site support burden.

🤖

AI-Assisted Triage & Routing

The Hermes AI agent handles first-contact triage for help desk requests submitted through the client portal or the on-site chat widget. Hermes classifies the request, assigns an SLA priority, routes it to the appropriate specialist queue, and sends the user an acknowledgment with the ticket number and expected response time. For common request types — password resets, VPN connectivity, software access — Hermes resolves the ticket automatically with Honcho-managed context keeping conversations coherent across sessions.

👥

Onboarding & Offboarding

New hire and departure workflows are templated in Freshdesk with defined task sequences. A new hire ticket automatically triggers account creation in Active Directory, Microsoft 365 license assignment, device configuration, and application access provisioning — all tracked in a single parent ticket with subtasks per system. Offboarding reverses the process: AD account disabled, M365 license revoked, device wiped, and access audit completed within one business day of the departure date.

Three Ways to Reach Support

Users submit tickets through the self-service portal, via SMS text-in, or through the Hermes chat widget embedded in the client portal. Every channel feeds directly into Freshdesk with the same SLA rules applied regardless of submission method.

The Hermes AI agent handles triage and routing automatically — so tickets arrive in the right queue with the right priority without a dispatcher manually touching each one. Technicians open Freshdesk and find a pre-classified, pre-prioritized queue ready to work.

Automated remediation handles the most common request types without human intervention: password resets, VPN reconnections, disk cleanup, and software reinstalls are resolved in seconds with zero touch from the help desk team.
