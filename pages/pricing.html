Transition Program

Service Transition Plan

The transition from signed agreement to live managed service is a structured six-week program with defined milestones, formal handoff points, and sign-off requirements at each phase boundary. Nothing goes live until the prior phase is fully closed and accepted. Every deliverable is documented before the next phase starts, so both teams have a clear shared understanding of what was built, what was handed off, and who owns what going forward.

Post-Go-Live Change Management

All changes to the managed environment after go-live go through a formal change management process with documented approval before any work is performed.

  • All non-emergency changes submitted through Freshdesk before work begins
  • Emergency changes handled under P1 SLA and fully documented within 24 hours
  • Scheduled patches deployed automatically via NinjaOne and JAMF on defined windows
  • Major infrastructure changes require IT Manager approval and a rollback plan
  • PRTG monitors all sites continuously and alerts on any post-change anomaly
  • Change log maintained in Ninja Docs and reviewed in the monthly operations call

Phase Handoff Points

Each phase of the transition ends with a formal sign-off process before the next phase begins. Both sides confirm all deliverables are complete, tested, and accepted.

  • Phase completion documented and countersigned by the client IT Manager
  • No next phase starts until the prior phase is fully closed and signed off
  • Each handoff includes a walkthrough of what was built, configured, and tested
  • Site coordinators at all seven locations confirm local readiness before cutover
  • Outstanding items are documented as tracked exceptions, not waived

Knowledge Transfer & Self-Service

End users and IT staff receive structured onboarding to the new tools and support channels before go-live, so day one isn't a learning curve for anyone.

  • Self-service portal and pop-up widget configured and tested before go-live
  • SMS text-in support channel active from day one
  • Hermes AI agent trained on your environment and FAQ content before launch
  • Freshdesk automation rules and SLA policies reviewed with the client team
  • Help desk staff trained and certified on all tools before the first ticket
  • Runbooks for top 20 request types documented in Ninja Docs at handoff
